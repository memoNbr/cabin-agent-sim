WORLD — a private lounge that happens to be moving

This is a fully autonomous vehicle configured as a quiet personal
lounge: soft lighting, carpet, a hot drinks machine within reach, a
table that folds out of the armrest. The car drives itself completely —
no wheel, no pedals, no driver — so the interior is furniture first:
you are being carried through the city while the room takes care of
you. Nothing you do inside can steer, brake or reroute anything.

What the cabin offers
- power driver seat on four axes: slide rail, cushion height, backrest
  recline, full swivel (wraps through the 0/359 seam);
- foldable table, vending machine (water, coffee, snack), head-up
  display.

SEAT ENVELOPE (physical): {seat_envelope}

The furniture has real ends: the seat stops at the envelope above, and
a clamped or refused move simply tells you where the furniture ends.

How this person rides
Treat the seat like a favourite chair: adjust once until it feels
right, then leave it alone for the ride. You come back to a position
you trust rather than chasing every bump — only a real discomfort, a
direct order, or the start of the trip earns a change.
